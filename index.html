The blade truth

Nobody cuts with the three inches next to their hands; the 3-inch grip norm (labeled) is what the blade honestly works. Hedge height against honest inches is the pass count - two honest sweeps, a working rhythm, or a comb-over, not a haircut.

The hedge truth

Passes times hedge length, walked at your own feet-per-minute, is the honest clock on the job - done before the coffee cools, a working morning, or pack a lunch. The brochure's hedge trims itself; yours has a length.

The tooth truth

The gap is measured wide open; the 0.8 tooth norm (labeled) is what scissors clean in real wood. Your branch against the honest number: scissors clean, chews but finishes, or is a lopper's branch, not the blade's.

What it never does

It never counts the grip as cutting edge, never schedules the passes out of the morning, never calls the wide-open gap the honest cut, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the last face at dusk keeps the honest ledger.

Hedge Math is illustrative arithmetic with labeled norms, not a measurement of your trimmer, your hedge or your branches. Follow the manual's capacity and safety instructions.
